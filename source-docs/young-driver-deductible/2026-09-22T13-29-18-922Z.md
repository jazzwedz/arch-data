# 1. Business needs / Business requirements

## 1.1 Business value

**What is the added business value?**

1. **Risk Differentiation and Fair Premium Allocation**
   Statistically, young drivers (under `[AGE THRESHOLD]`) are more likely to be involved in
   accidents. A separate deductible reflects this higher risk and ensures a fairer distribution of
   premiums across customer segments.
2. **Reduction in Claims Costs**
   A higher deductible for young drivers lowers the insurer's share of claim payouts, improving the
   loss ratio and overall profitability.
3. **Behavioral Incentives**
   A higher financial stake in the event of a claim can encourage safer driving behavior among
   young drivers.
4. **Competitive Positioning**
   Many competitors (`[FOUR NAMED COMPETITORS REMOVED — naming them would identify the market]`)
   already apply separate deductibles for young drivers. Introducing our own model helps maintain
   competitiveness and allows for more tailored product offerings.
5. **Product Segmentation and Customization**
   Enables differentiated pricing and the development of targeted add-ons (e.g. bonus protection,
   safe driving programs), enhancing customer retention and satisfaction.

**What is the consequence if this change is not implemented?**

1. **Risk of Adverse Selection**
   Without a separate deductible, the insurer may attract a disproportionate number of high-risk
   young drivers, increasing the overall risk exposure of the portfolio.
2. **Competitive Disadvantage**
   Insurers without a young driver deductible may appear less risk-aware or less professional,
   especially compared to competitors with more refined pricing models.
3. **Higher Claims Costs**
   The insurer bears the full cost of young driver claims, which can negatively impact the
   profitability of the motor insurance line.
4. **Lack of Behavioral Control**
   Without financial incentives, there is less motivation for young drivers to adopt safer driving
   habits, potentially leading to higher claim frequencies.

## 1.2 Description

**What is this change about?**

Implementing a new deductible for young drivers for passenger cars, motorcycles and delivery trucks
in Motor Insurance.

The date of birth of the policyholder/owner should be validated:

1. If the policyholder/owner is under `[AGE THRESHOLD]`, the system view should remain unchanged and
   the chosen regular deductible shall apply (status quo for all systems — no application of any new
   tariff factor).
2. If the policyholder/owner is over `[AGE THRESHOLD]`, a new query on the driver's circle is
   displayed and, depending on the answer (Yes / No), …
   - **Yes:** … an additional input field for the date of birth is also displayed; the chosen regular
     deductible shall apply (status quo) and a separate deductible for drivers under
     `[AGE THRESHOLD]` shall be automatically stored for liability and collision, with a dropdown
     similar to the standard deductible. The selection must be at least equal to the regular
     deductible value or higher. Example of the system view (translations below):
     *Screen CORE for Offer, Provisional policy and Policy (SEC-ADMIN and WEB at the end of the
     document).* Re-use of the input block used until the `[OLDER TARIFF GENERATION]` for the
     driver's circle, including validation of the inputs but with adaptation of the questions.
     `[FIGURES REMOVED — 3 screenshots of the offer / provisional policy / policy screens in CORE]`
   - **No:** … the system view remains unchanged; the chosen regular deductible shall apply (status
     quo) and a separate deductible for drivers under `[AGE THRESHOLD]` shall be automatically stored
     for liability and collision and be greyed out. The young driver deductibles for liability and

**Explanation of options a. and b. under point 2**

When calculating online premiums, neither `[PRICE COMPARISON PORTAL REMOVED]` nor the websites of
our competitors ask for information (name, address, etc.) about drivers under `[AGE THRESHOLD]`.
Accordingly, a separate query was shown in the example mock-ups.

To promote correct application recording, a question should be asked whether drivers under
`[AGE THRESHOLD]` will drive the insured vehicle. If "No" is answered, the young driver deductibles
for liability and collision shall be fixed and not changeable. If "Yes" is answered, the young
driver deductibles for liability and collision shall be selectable in analogy to the standard
deductible, but the selection of the young driver deductibles may differ.

After initial discussions the default values for the young driver deductible are to be stored as
follows (subject to change):

> `[DEDUCTIBLE DEFAULT MATRIX REMOVED — this is a tariff/underwriting rule.]`
> **Structure retained so the requirement stays reviewable, values removed:** a table with one row
> per standard deductible band (6 bands) for each of two guarantees (liability, collision) —
> 12 rows — and three columns: *Standard deductible*, *Young driver declared (answer: Yes)*,
> *Young driver not declared (answer: No)*. The "declared" cell gives a default young driver
> deductible plus the further options selectable above it; the "not declared" cell gives a single
> fixed value, greyed out and not selectable. Two floor rules accompanied the table, both of the
> form *"if the standard deductible is raised to band X, the young driver deductible must also be
> raised to band X"*, one for the declared and one for the not-declared case, at different bands.

**Impact for Claims**

In the event of a loss event, the driver, including the date of birth, is already recorded in the
system for the claim settlement.

To avoid making the claims settlement more complex for the Claims colleagues, a validation should be
implemented that considers the date of birth of the driver recorded when creating a claim for
policies with the `[NEW TARIFF GENERATION]` and newer, and automatically applies the correct
deductible.

Depending on the driver recorded, the deductible (regular or young driver deductible) is only
calculated once per guarantee and should not be added up.

For the sake of simplicity, the current designation of the deductible — "contractual deductible" —
should remain unchanged in the text of the documents (e.g. the invoice).

In the following three possible constellations:

1. If the policyholder is under `[AGE THRESHOLD]`, the standard deductible applies regardless of the
   driver recorded and their age.
2. If the policyholder is over `[AGE THRESHOLD]` and the driver recorded is also over
   `[AGE THRESHOLD]`, the standard deductible applies.
3. If the policyholder is over `[AGE THRESHOLD]` but the driver recorded is under
   `[AGE THRESHOLD]`, the young driver deductible applies.
